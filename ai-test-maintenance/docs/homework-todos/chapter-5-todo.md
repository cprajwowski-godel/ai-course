Try It Yourself
In this final exercise, you’ll practice applying AI to a small collection of outdated specs and bring them up to professional standards.

Preconditions:

At least three outdated or redundant specs in tests/ folder.
Professionalized navigation test from Chapter 4 as a reference.
Instruction for AI assistant:

Review all specs in the tests/ folder.
Identify broken selectors, redundant scenarios, or obsolete logic.
Suggest a plan to consolidate overlapping tests.
Generate a diff for one representative file showing recommended cleanup.
Summarize results as a short markdown report titled suite-maintenance-summary.md.
Your task:

Ask AI to perform the scan and review its findings.
Approve only the changes that improve readability and reduce duplication.
Refactor one file completely following AI’s plan; keep others for manual review.
Record before-and-after results in docs/suite-maintenance-summary.md.
Deliverables
Updated and cleaned-up specs inside tests/.
docs/suite-maintenance-summary.md – AI findings + your final decisions.
Playwright HTML report showing successful suite execution.
